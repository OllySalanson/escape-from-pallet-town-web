# Inside the next gym, the chip names its leader

On a raid with no job the gold arrow leads to the next gym's door. Once the player was through it, the arrow turned green and pointed back out to the nearest exit, and the chip read `EXTRACT VIA DEER GAP: S`, with KOGA standing at the end of the room. Now the chip reads `KOGA: N` and no arrow is drawn: the room is the gym's own puzzle, and its trainers stand on the ways through it.

- `before.png`: main, a few steps inside FUCHSIA GYM.
- `after.png`: the same spot after the change.

Made with `tools/playtest/ai-player/act` on a test-mode build (`?testmode=1`, so no tints), from a save with MISTY beaten and a Lv 25 team, NO JOB on the rim.
