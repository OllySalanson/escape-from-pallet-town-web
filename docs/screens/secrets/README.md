# Secrets, first wave

The vale's first seven secrets (`src/game/world/secrets.ts`): a nook, a clue
beside it, and a condition fixed as the raid deploys. Played with
`tools/playtest/raid.mjs --clock=dusk|day|night --insertion=greystone-vale-moor-top`.

1. THE LONG SHADOW's clue at the foot of the tor, read in the afternoon.
2. At dusk, nine steps east where the shadow ends: a piece of the torn chart.
3. The same tile by day: nothing there (`secrets open []`).
4. THE LOST FOLD's clue, outside the Strength boulder that shuts the fold.
5. At night, with the boulder pushed: the fold's piece.
6. The piece in the pack, at risk until it is carried home.
7. The finds' icons, 12x: three torn corners of one chart, the Helix and Dome Fossils, and Old Amber beside them for scale.
