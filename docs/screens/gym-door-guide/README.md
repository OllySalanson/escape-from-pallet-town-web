# The level guide at a gym door

The last box of each gym's door sign, as it reads in a raid: worked out from
the leader's own team (`world/gymGuide.ts`). Before this change the sign
stopped at the box before.

- `cerulean.png`: `MISTY: three Pokémon, Lv 13 to 15, two out at once. Bring two at Lv 15 or more.`
- `fuchsia.png`: `KOGA: three Pokémon, Lv 17 to 20, two out at once. Bring two at Lv 20 or more. KOFFING knows SELF-DESTRUCT!`

Made on 22 Nov 2026 against the dev server in `?testmode=pixels`: a raid
deployed through `tools/playtest/deploy.mjs`, the player stood facing each sign
and SPACE pressed through its boxes.
