# The captain's spyglass

Round 12 of the badass harbour: a reason to walk to the end of the long pier, something to do
after dark, and a few of the vale's secrets.

## What changed

- **The S.S. Anne's captain lends his spyglass.** Speak to him on the long pier and he hands it
  over; what you see depends on the hour by your own clock (`src/game/base/spyglass.ts`).
- **By day**, the sea, the Seagallop's wake, the S.S. Anne - and the captain telling you to come
  back after dark.
- **At dusk**, the sun going down behind Greystone Vale and the first lights on the far coast.
- **After dark**, one look at a time, the lights of the vale's far places - the ones a raid has to
  work hardest to reach - each with the one true thing you need to know about getting there:
  Stormcrown (only from the deepest cave floor, and a climb), the Moor Chapel (the fog lifts only
  after dark, along the sheep track over High Moor), Ember Isle (only the smugglers' skiff),
  Frostreef (a boat from the wreck's pier, on the tide), the Lost Garden (a cave behind the falls by
  the power station) and the Bunker (lights under the power station, behind a Card Key door).
  `spyglass.test.ts` holds every one to a place the vale's own plan names.

## How to use it

Walk out along the long pier and talk to the captain. Go back after dark, and keep looking.
