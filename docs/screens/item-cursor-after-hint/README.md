# After the low-HP line, the menu comes back on ITEM

"SQUIRTLE is low on HP! Choose ITEM to give it a POTION." is said once a fight (#681). The commands then came back on FIGHT, so the SPACE after the line attacked, and an AI player's Bulbasaur on 5/32 went down to Starmie with Potions packed. Now they come back on ITEM, as KEEP THE PACK returns to BALL (#718); from the next turn the cursor is wherever the player last chose.

- `after.png`: a Squirtle on 4/19 meets a wild Meowth; SPACE through the hint, then one more SPACE opens the Potion list.

Made with `tools/playtest/ai-player/act` on a test-mode build (`?testmode=1`), from a save whose Squirtle was set to 4 HP.
