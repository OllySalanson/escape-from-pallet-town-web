# The regulars and T phrases, watched at length

A focused playtest of THE HARBOUR's twelve regulars (activities, arrivals, doors,
speech bubbles) and the player's own T phrases, at 1200x768 and 640x480.
`tools/playtest/phrasesPlayed.mjs` plays what was wrong; `regularsActivities.mjs`
is the long watch.

- `before-doorstep-empty.png`, `before-doorstep-regular-beside.png`: nothing named on
  the Pokémon Center's own step, with or without a regular. `after-doorstep.png` and
  `after-doorstep-small-window.png` are the same step now.
- `before-note-over-bubble.png`, `after-note-beside-bubble.png`: the partner's note
  over this player's own bubble, and the bubble seated clear of it.
- `after-t-while-walking.png`: T tapped mid-step, the arrow still held.
- `chat.png`, `knock.png`, `arriving-off-the-seagallop.png`,
  `phrase-replaces-a-name.png`: what was right, for reference.
