# Favours from people about the harbour

The captain, 29 Oct: "maybe there can be people walking around ... and you can
speak to them and they can give you missions". The four keepers ask theirs
indoors (`../favours-face-to-face/`); these two are out in the yard, asked in
the same dialogue box with the same YES or NOT NOW, and with nothing to open
after it. Both are FireRed's own errands, with FireRed's own rewards.

| Who | Where they stand | Wants | Where it lies | Squares | Pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Captain Hale, the S.S. Anne's captain | the long pier | his old ship's log | THE WRECK | 2 | HM01 Cut (FireRed's captain gives it) |
| Warden Fenwick, the Safari Zone Warden | walking the green, by the bench above the cove | his GOLD TEETH | SAFARI MARSH | 1 | HM04 Strength (FireRed's warden gives it) |

A "!" walks about over anybody who has something to say about a favour, and
comes down once it is answered. The Warden, as in FireRed, cannot be
understood until his teeth are back in: his favour is told in brackets, and his
everyday lines are gibberish until then.

Shot at 3x (1200x768) against a test-mode build with the roster as shipped, by
`tools/playtest/harbourFavours.mjs`, which plays each beat and fails on any
that is not what it promises.
