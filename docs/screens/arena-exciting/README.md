# The Raid Arena, made more exciting

The third Arena sweep, played as a player: the fear was real but lived in one row of a panel, and a jump had no held breath.

- `two-press-team-on-bench.png`: home, SPACE, and a team of two is on the bench as it was left. It used to read "Nobody is on the bench" for anyone with more than one Pokémon.
- `held-breath-new-team.png`: on the rail with the fires low and the hall silent, a new team's first raids name everything at risk.
- `held-breath-veteran.png`: after three raids with a team, only the biggest thing, at the 320x240 stage.
- `pursuer-hanging.png`: a few moments into the fall, the raid's hunter (Sabrina here) has come over the rail after them, and goes on down past them.
- `run-slate.png`: the slate on the rail by the runner.
- `run-struck.png`: a lost raid strikes it through in front of the groan, then it is wiped.
- `close-call-crowd.png`: out with the clock nearly gone. The crowd is up and the board says so. (Taken before the line read the enrage's clock, so it says 0:00 where the build now says what the chip read.)
- `close-call-result.png`: the same line on the result screen.

Made against a test-mode build through `?testmode=pixels`, on a hand-seeded returning save.
