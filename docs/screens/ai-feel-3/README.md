The remaining lines the four AI playtesters flagged as "ai-feel", rewritten in the keepers' and townsfolk's own voices (`docs/ai-feel/README.md` says who stays formal):

- THE GAME CORNER's attendant used to read out nine lines of rate tables. Now she welcomes you, says which cabinet is which, and says each cabinet's screen gives its rates. The rates moved to the cabinet's own screen, as one line under the team (`cabinetRates`).
- Hunters' openers: MISTY and SABRINA each gloat about your loot in their own way, where before all of them shared one joke.
- MISTY's badge line, Mrs Gantry, the Stone Carter, Netter Bryn, Master Pemberton and the leftover Bill lines are now contracted. THE GREAT LIBRARY's librarian no longer reads out a key binding.
