# The woodcutter's ride, dry from end to end

The overnight feature tester met a wild fight on five runs of five down the Cut line - it waded six
wild tiles - so GOLD and the day's purse were a coin flip for the player who did the clever thing.
Now the line is dry: the bed west of the millpond stops short of the bank (`1-dry-bank-by-the-millpond.png`,
the player on the trodden bank by the woodshed) and the last wade to THE LAST HOUSE is a trodden strip
beside its wall (`2-dry-way-down-to-the-last-house.png`). Straight through the open reeds - the floor,
PERFECT - is still a wade.

Played three times with a held start (`node tools/playtest/race.mjs "$URL" --hold --line=cut
--opened=greystone-vale/door-2`): 68 steps, 0 wild, no fight, GOLD 0:10.2 each time. Pictures from a
test-mode build at `?testmode=pixels`, 1200x768 (`--pixels --shot-every=800`).
