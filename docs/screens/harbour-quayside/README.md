# Quayside polish, and a ship's horn across the water

Round 9 of the badass harbour: a pass over what still looked unfinished.

## What changed

- **The quay corner** the harbour light left when it moved to the breakwater has crates off the
  Seagallop on it, minded by a PORTER who talks about them (`harbourLife.ts`).
- **The crate tower by the cove** carried a tuft of lawn baked into its art onto the stone quay;
  it is the clean crate stack now.
- **The S.S. Anne sounds her horn** now and then (every 95-190 s), one low blast from out in the
  harbour, heard anywhere outdoors (`distantHorn`).
- Every walk unchanged; `harbourLife.test.ts` holds the porter to the same rules as everyone else.

## How to use it

Talk to the porter at the west end of the quay. Stand about outdoors a couple of minutes and
listen for the liner.
