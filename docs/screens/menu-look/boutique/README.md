# THE HARBOUR BOUTIQUE and the wardrobe

The round-3 mock-up's boutique (Olly kept "the head, top, legs, full kit section"):

- The mirror: the try-on big in the glass, HEAD, TOP, BOTTOMS and OUTFIT cards beside it
  (a gold head is a piece not yours yet), and along its foot what you have on now beside
  the try-on turning round.
- The shelves one at a time behind a pager (Q and E, Page Up and Page Down, or its
  arrows), each card's money a gold tag and its parts as tags. A screen too low for two
  rows of cards draws their figures at the art's own size (`BoutiqueScene.fitShelf`).
- IT TAKES along the foot: each cost a tag with HAVE under it, green when there is
  enough, grey when not; ENTER BUY and PUT IT BACK.

`before-shop.png` is the shop before; `after-trying-on.png` has Champion Red tried on at
1280x800 under the controls bar; `after-wardrobe.png` is the wardrobe at home. Made with a
small driver on `tools/playtest/browser.mjs` (open the boutique, Right, Enter), and
`tools/playtest/boutique.mjs` plays the whole shop by the keys.
