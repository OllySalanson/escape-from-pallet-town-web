# The fight's inks

A command that cannot be chosen (HEAL at full health, an empty stack, a fainted Pokémon) was drawn in red, which in the menus' look means something that can be lost; it is grey now, as every button that cannot be used is. The guidance under a list is ink rather than slate blue, a good matchup the safe green and a bad one red, the status tag red, and the strip under the panel the menus' ink rather than navy. `scenes/BattleScene.ts`.

Before is the fight after the foundation re-skin (#895); after is this change. Made with `tools/playtest/battleKeys.mjs <test-mode build> <dir>` (its second picture).
