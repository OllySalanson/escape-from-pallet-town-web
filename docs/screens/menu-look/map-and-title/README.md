# THE MAP's title strip and the title's prompt

- THE MAP (M) had a title line of its own; it is every screen's strip now - ink, the Poke Ball's red seam, a drawn ball before the name, the raid clock in a white pill, the X a white square.
- The title screen's prompt plate and its buttons' shadow were the dusk's navy; they are the menus' ink. The dusk over the town is the scene's own and stays.

`before-title.png` was caught during the title's fade-in, which is why its buttons look grey; `after-title.png` is after it. Made with `tools/playtest/menusAudit.mjs <build> <out> --only=the-map` and a small title driver, at 1280x800.
