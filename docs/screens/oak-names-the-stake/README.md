# Oak names the stake the gold arrow is after

A new save's first raid carries no job, so the gold arrow leads to the raid's stake (`TAKE ₽10 ON THE WAY TO ₽75: E`). Oak's radio and the field guide said nothing of it: their first line was the nearest landmark, the VILLAGE PUMP to the west. A friend who had never played was pointed east by the arrow and west by Oak on their first minute.
