# Getting ready for a raid in THE RAID ARENA's hall

Everything before a raid is done on the floor of the great hall. Photographed by
`tools/playtest/arenaHall.mjs` (`--tour`, `--window=`, `--still`) against a test-mode build.
Where everything stands and why: `src/game/base/arenaHall.ts` and `arenaRim.ts`.

- `1-arrival.png`: through the door. Nobody speaks; the marshal has a "!" until she has been spoken to.
- `2-marshal.png`: what the room is, said once and only when asked.
- `3-bench.png`, `bench-with-a-team.png`: who comes, in a panel over the room, and sitting on the stone afterwards.
- `4-rack.png`: what is packed, how it is laid out, and which pack. `5-locker.png`: what comes home whatever happens.
- `6-rim.png`, `6-rim-north.png`: on the rim the hint says which way in is under you, and it is marked in the pit.
- `7-rim-words.png`, `8-looking-down.png`: looking down, with the marshal's words the first time.
- `many-ways-in.png`: a save with more ways in: a row under the cursor swings the view round to it.
- `a-players-map-in-the-pit.png`: a way in on another map puts that map in the pit.
- `nobody-on-the-bench.png`: the rim still opens, and JUMP IN says why not.
- `9-jump-*.png`, `10-landing-1.png`, `11-landed.png`: over the fence, down through the cloud, and out of it.
- `12-fog-lifting.png`, `13-fog-lifted.png`: home from a raid, back on the rim, with the cloud coming off what it walked.
- `small-window-*.png`, `large-window-*.png`: 640x480 and 1920x1080.
