# YOUR TEAM on a screen short of height

At 800x512 the panel is a whole-screen sheet and still too short for the card under the boxes: before, its name and HP were under the frame and its LEAD tick was drawn on the hint line. After, on a screen short of height (`data-short`, which `MenuOverlay` already sets below 300 game pixels, `isShortRoom`) the card drops its big portrait (the slot above shows the icon) and puts its type badge and where it lives on one line. Both after pictures have 18 in the boxes; 1280x720 is a side column of 360 and unchanged. Made against a test-mode build on `?testmode=pixels` in a headless Chromium at each size, pressing P in the harbour.
