Bill's greeting said "Bank 3 jobs off Oak's board, or beat a boss, and I'll sell you more" while his list's header counts "0 POINTS · 6 MORE FOR REGULAR". An AI friend could not tell whether 3 jobs was 6 points or a second rule. He now says "Bank 3 jobs off Oak's board - 2 points apiece on my list - or beat a boss, and I'll sell you more.", cut into two boxes at the first dash.
