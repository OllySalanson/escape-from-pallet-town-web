# The Raid Arena, made cooler

The first of the sweeps over THE RAID ARENA's great hall. Photographed against a test-mode build at the 3x window; hours by time zone (`Emulation.setTimezoneOverride`), walked ground from a sample survey.

- `the-fall.png`: the jump, frame by frame. The player and partner stay big on the screen, spinning, with wind and torn cloud (`base/arenaDive.ts`, `base/arenaDiveView.ts`).
- `ring-of-fire.png`: the torches flaring as they go over (`base/ringOfFire.ts`).
- `the-landing.png`: falling into a still picture, then dust and the partner's bounce (`world/raidArrival.ts`).
- `the-shadow.png`, `eyes-in-a-gate.png`: what lives in the pit (`base/pitLife.ts`). Rare, and never explained.
- `arrival-with-stands.png`, `north-wall-looking-up.png`, `east-wall.png`: the seats, the crowd, the arcade, the banners and the heavier fence (`base/arenaStands.ts`, `scripts/draw-arena-hall.mts`).
- `rim-by-day.png`, `rim-at-dusk.png`, `night-town-lights.png`: the pit by the player's clock (`base/pitClock.ts`).
