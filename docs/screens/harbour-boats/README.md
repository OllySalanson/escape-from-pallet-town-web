# The boats ride the water, and the Seagallop brings you home

Round 5 of the badass harbour.

## What changed

- **The boats ride the swell.** The Seagallop and the S.S. Anne used to be painted on the sea like
  anything else on the map. Once the harbour is on screen their tiles are lifted off the map and
  drawn again as one picture each that rises and falls a pixel on a swell of its own - a quick one
  under the ferry, a long slow one under the liner (`src/game/base/floatingBoats.ts`).
- **The Seagallop brings a raid home.** Come home from a raid and you watch her come in from the
  bay, easing into her berth beside the jetty you've just stepped onto, horn sounding.
  `clip-seagallop-arrives.png` is the arrival at real speed.
- Nothing about where anybody walks moved: each hull's tiles stay solid where the map put them.

## How to use it

Come home from a raid and look down at the water by the jetty. Then walk out along the long pier
and watch the S.S. Anne breathe.
