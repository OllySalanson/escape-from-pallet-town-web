# The Raid Arena, tidied

The fourth Arena sweep, which was the one to make sure earlier sweeps had not gone overboard. It played door to landing to home at raid 1 and raid 200, and asked of each effect whether it earned its place on every raid after the first.

- `before-after.png`: the changes that show in a picture, each before beside after.
- `reel-question.png`: once the trailer has been seen the door goes straight to the hall, and the Gym Guide runs it again if asked, with the cursor on NOT NOW.
- `home-roar.png`: the look round at the crowd on coming home, now only for a roar or a groan, stopping short of the dark under the stands.

The rest of it is sound and timing, which a picture cannot show:

- The crowd and the wind of a fall have a channel of their own (`hall`). A partner's chirp used to cut a lost raid's groan off about a tenth of a second in.
- The torch crackle was the tall-grass rustle, up to 23 times a minute. It is its own sound now, every 15 to 40 seconds, and never over a box, a panel or the crowd.
- The hunter's arrival sting no longer plays on every jump. The hunter still falls after the player, silently.
- Walking the rim to choose a way in no longer gets a cheer every quarter of the ring.
- The press that cuts a fog lift short now also does what it was pressed for, so home, SPACE, ENTER is true.

`tools/playtest/arenaIntro.mjs`, `arenaHall.mjs` and `arenaFun.mjs` play all of it.
