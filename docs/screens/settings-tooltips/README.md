# Map tooltips on and off

Olly: "on there you should be able to turn tooltips off. Tooltips are the boxes that hover over the top of the map explaining what everything is."

1. The harbour with tooltips on: POKEMON CENTER names itself as the player stands by it.
2. SETTINGS (the controls bar under the game): MAP TOOLTIPS turned OFF.
3. After a reload: no label speaks on its own - the choice is kept by the browser, like the mute.
4. With L held, every label on the screen still names itself, so nobody loses their way.

Made with a small driver on `tools/playtest/browser.mjs` at 1280x800 against a test-mode build.
