# The trade question puts the cursor on KEEP IT

THE TRADER, a Kadabra, TRADE AND BACK pressed once.

- `before.png`: main at c1c14088. The bar asks "SEND KADABRA OVER AND BACK?", but focus stayed on the card's own TRADE AND BACK chip, scrolled out of sight under MORE. ENTER or SPACE again only asked again.
- `after.png`: this change. The cursor is on KEEP IT, as on Bill's shelf and barters. A second SPACE keeps the Kadabra and returns to its row; TRADE AND BACK is one arrow away.

Made with the playtest harness on a test-mode build at 800x512. Focus was read with `document.activeElement`.
