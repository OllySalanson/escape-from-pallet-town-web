# Favours, asked face to face

The captain, 29 Oct: being given a quest was "an overlay when you go into a
house and speak to someone ... it ruins the flow of the game. It's difficult
to see what's going on, there's too much writing". He asked for "a normal
Pokémon chat": somebody sets the scene, says what to do, and you accept or
decline.

**Before.** Speaking to a keeper opened their whole menu screen with the
favour laid over its foot - two or three long sentences on top of a counter of
numbers (the Pokémon Center is 205 words, Bill's cottage 712) - and one button,
GOT IT. The favour was taken the moment the screen opened, before a word of it
was read, and said once only. The hand-over and the loss came the same way.

- `../errands/1-joy-asks.png` - Nurse Joy's favour over the Pokémon Center.
- `before-brock-thanks.png` - the crate handed over, as the fourth line of a box
  over Brock's ladder of upgrades.
- `before-oak-lost.png` - Oak asking after a lost parcel, over his lab.

**After.** The keeper says it in the room's own dialogue box - the box the
harbour's sailor and angler already speak in - one box a line, and ends on a
question with YES and NOT NOW in the box's own frame. Only YES takes the
favour; NOT NOW takes nothing and is asked again after the next raid. Then the
keeper's screen opens, because that is what the key was pressed for. A keeper
with something to say wears FireRed's "!".

- `1-brock-mark.png` - Brock with a favour on his mind.
- `2-brock-asks.png`, `3-brock-asks.png` - the scene, then what to fetch and
  what it costs the pack.
- `4-brock-question.png` - the question, YES first as FireRed's box is.
- `5-brock-accepted.png` - his one line to a YES; his screen opens after it.
- `6-joy-not-now.png`, `7-joy-declined.png` - NOT NOW, and what she says to it.
- `8-brock-handover.png`, `9-brock-received.png` - the crate home: handed over
  and paid in the same box.
