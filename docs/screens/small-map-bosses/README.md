# The two small-map doors, in the real game at 3x

Photographed with `tools/playtest/tour.mjs` in `?testmode=pixels` at the 3x
`PIXEL_WINDOW`, which is the only window a caption should be judged in.

| Shot | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `pallet-towpath-gate-shut.png` | Pallet Town, a fresh save, standing on the pond lane at 26,12. `TOWPATH GATE / HELD BY MILLER VANCE` over the hurdle, the miller's watch warning over the one tile in front of him, and the lane east free. |
| `pallet-towpath-steps-open.png` | The same town with him beaten, at the foot of the towpath. `TOWPATH STEPS / OPEN`, and the Sluice Wheel - the landmark that opens the West Culvert - captioned in the same frame: that is what the second door buys. |
| `forest-ridge-gate-shut.png` | Viridian Forest, a fresh save, on the trail under the fire tower at 18,6. `RIDGE GATE + STAIR / HELD BY LOOKOUT PELL` - one keeper, one caption - with the lookout on the nub above and the rock crest along the top of the wood behind him. |
| `forest-ridge-open.png` | The ridge itself with him beaten: bare ground, no tall grass anywhere on it, the signal flag on the crest, and `RIDGE GATE + STAIR / OPEN`. |
