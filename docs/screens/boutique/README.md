# THE HARBOUR BOUTIQUE

What the player wears (`src/game/look/`), and the shop and the wardrobe it is
bought and changed in. Every picture is a test-mode build at the game's 3x
window; the shop ones are `tools/playtest/boutique.mjs`, the two-player one
`tools/playtest/sharedHarbour.mjs` and the regular `tools/playtest/harbourRegulars.mjs`.

- `the-green-at-night.png` - the boutique at the top of THE GREEN, Vermilion's
  Fan Club under its green awning, set into the trees; the door's caption counts
  what the save owns. The player has just walked out in Champion Red.
- `shop-mirror.png` - pointing at a card: the mirror walks in it, in all four
  facings, and the list under it says what it is made of and what each piece
  costs. Pointing commits nothing.
- `shop-trying-on.png` - ENTER tried it on: the bar along the foot says what is
  new and what it comes to, in Pokédollars and found goods, and buys it.
- `shop-bought.png` - bought and worn, paid out of the vault in one write.
- `wardrobe.png` - YOUR WARDROBE, the drawers upstairs in THE BOLTHOLE: only
  what is owned, the classic look always among it.
- `walk-every-facing.png` - the bought look walking the yard: down, right, up and
  left, a frame every three, step and idle.
- `head-mark-on-the-head.png` - the chevron over the player sits on the look's
  own head; a FireRed cap starts lower than the classic crop
  (`lookHeadDrop`), and so do another player's name plate and wave.
- `another-player-in-a-look.png` - two tabs over the local harbour: AMY sees
  OLLY in a red cap, an Ace Trainer's jacket and jeans, his Bulbasaur at heel.
- `regular-in-sailor-whites.png` - ARLO, one of the twelve regulars, dressed
  from the boutique's shelves, answering the player beside the boutique's door.
- `smallest-screen.png` - the shop at the smallest stage (640x480): a narrower
  mirror walking at two game pixels a pixel, and cards whose figures are the
  game's own size, two across, each folding whole under the MORE strip.
