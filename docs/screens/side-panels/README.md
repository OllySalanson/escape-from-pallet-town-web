# The raid's screens at the side

The pack, the party, reading a disc and the harbour's favours used to be full screens with half of each one empty.
They are side panels now, the FEEDBACK panel's shape, with the map still there beside them (`src/game/ui/sidePanel.ts`).
Photographed by `tools/playtest/sidePanels.mjs` and `tools/playtest/favourList.mjs` against a test-mode build, at 1280x800 unless named.

- `before-*.png`: the screens as they were, from the menu screens playtest.
- `0-raid.png`: the raid with nothing open.
- `1-pack.png`, `2-pack-who.png`: the pack, and who a Potion is for.
- `4-teach.png`, `5-teach-chosen.png`, `5b-move-chooser.png`: reading a disc, the card chosen, and the question a full moveset asks.
- `7-party.png`, `10-party-gear.png`: the party, and TAB across to give gear.
- `11-pack-east-edge.png`: at the map's east edge the map stops at the panel, and the player is still beside it.
- `12-pack-hunted.png`: a hunter closing in. The clock, the hunter and its chip stand where they stood until the panel shuts.
- `favours.png`: the favours notebook, beside the harbour.
- `small-window-*.png`: 960x600, where the clock stands under the objective.
