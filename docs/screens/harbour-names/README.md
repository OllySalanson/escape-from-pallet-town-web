# The harbour says where you are, and has a square at its heart

Round 4 of the badass harbour.

## What changed

- **FireRed's map-name board.** Arrive in the harbour - off the title screen or off the boat - and
  a little framed board drops in at the top left saying THE HARBOUR, rests a couple of seconds and
  goes back up, as FireRed names a town. Walk into another part and its name drops in:
  ARENA SQUARE, KEEPERS' ROW, HARBOUR SQUARE, THE QUAY, THE DOCKS, THE LONG PIER, THE COVE,
  THE GREEN (`src/game/base/harbourPlaces.ts`, `src/game/base/placeNameSign.ts`). Coming back out
  of a door does not re-announce anything.
- **HARBOUR SQUARE.** The bare sand rectangle across the middle of the yard is gone: KEEPERS' ROW
  is a two-tile path in front of the buildings, and below it a brick square with a fountain opens
  straight onto the quay, with lawns either side. A FireRed town map board at its corner says where
  everything is. THE GREEN's fountain is Celadon's round one now, so the two fountains differ.
- Every walk is the same length as before (yard: Arena 9, Center 5, Lab 11, Workshop 17, Bill 14,
  Bolthole 24; boat: Arena 11, Bill 4).

## How to use it

Start the game or come home from a raid and watch the top left. Walk about: each part of the
harbour names itself as you step into it. Read the town map at the square's corner for the lot.
