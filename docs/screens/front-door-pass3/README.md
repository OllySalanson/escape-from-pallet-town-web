# The front door after the critic's third pass

A fresh critic subagent played the whole rebuilt first half-hour (#835 to #852) and scored
it 7.5/10: "yes, I would now send a friend through this front door". This is its list of
six things that take it to 8.

- `before-chip-after-catch.png` / `after-chip-after-catch.png` - after the catch, the chip
  had already chosen the money while Oak said "your call". Now it says both, OUT: W · OR
  ₽75: NE, and the arrow is on the way out until the player is nearer the stake.
- `after-one-pidgey.png` - one Pidgey on the square, the arrow on it. The second (the
  retry) stands up only once the first has been fought.
- `before-card-footer.png` / `after-card-footer.png` - the moment's bar says SAVED (or the
  money banked) instead of a bare BANKED, and the key is on the button.

Also changed, in words only: Oak's lab no longer says ENRAGED before there is a clock. It
is said on the radio once per save, the first time a raid reaches a minute left. The
raid-2 offer reads "Your new catch is waiting at base. Take two, and one faint won't end
the raid." / "Take PIDGEY along?". The pack line reads "It takes 4 of the 18 squares."

`docs/screens/first-catch/`.
