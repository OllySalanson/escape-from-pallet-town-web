# Oak's gym line gives the leader's level

Oak's radio and the full list named the next gym, its way and its distance, then "MISTY is inside.", to a Lv 5 on a first raid. Her team leads at Lv 12, and the only place that said so was the CHALLENGE box at her door. Now the line ends "MISTY is inside, leading at Lv 12 to your Lv 5.", in the challenge box's own words.
