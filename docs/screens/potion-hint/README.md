# The medicine hint

Before: a Charmander at 2/18 HP with three Potions in the pack fought on and went down; nothing in the fight said a Potion could be used there. After: once a fight, when one of yours is at a quarter of its HP or less and the pack holds something that heals, the fight says so before the commands.

Made on a test-mode build (`VITE_EPTW_TEST_MODE=1`), `?testmode=1` in the 400x256 logic window, through `tools/playtest/ai-player/act` from a save whose Charmander was set to 2/18 HP, walking the arrow until a wild fight.
