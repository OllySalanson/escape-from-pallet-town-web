# Home storage: MY PC and THE BOLTHOLE's garden

MY PC upstairs in THE BOLTHOLE opens the same storage as BILL'S PC, with every thing in pockets, and up to six boxed Pokémon walk about the garden beside the house (`base/homeGarden.ts`).
Made by `tools/playtest/homeStorage.mjs` on a test-mode build at the 3x pixel window.

- `01b-door-caption.png` - the house's door says what is kept there; six boxed Pokémon in the garden; the Day-Care Man's name keeps clear of them.
- `03-take-it.png` - facing one says who it is and which box it lives in, then asks to take it on the next raid.
- `04-taken.png` - YES puts it on the team; the rest stay where they stood and the next one out comes out away from the player.
- `06-my-pc.png` - MY PC: storage headed as the player's own, the Pokédex in the title bar, things in pockets, each row saying where its Pokémon is.
- `07-my-pc-garden-chip.png` - the garden chip puts a Pokémon out (or brings one in).
- `09-bills-pc.png` - BILL'S PC at the Center: the same storage, the same pockets.
