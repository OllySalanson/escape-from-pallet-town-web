# The first catch, after the critic's second pass

On the first version (#843), BLUE came for the player while Oak was still talking, with
a team of two, and could take the Pidgey the tutorial had just taught them to catch. "Throw
a BALL now" also came one Scratch too late, with the menu still on FIGHT.

- `before-caught.png` - #843: BLUE ON YOUR TAIL already up under Oak's first box.
- `after-caught.png` - now: Oak says it first, with the pack's size ("taking 4 of its 18
  squares"). BLUE is sent after six steps of walking and brings one Pokémon.
- `after-throw-a-ball-now.png` - the lesson after one Scratch (6/13). The menu comes back
  on BALL.

Played on a test-mode build at 1200x768 with the same chip-following scratch driver as
`docs/screens/first-catch/`.
