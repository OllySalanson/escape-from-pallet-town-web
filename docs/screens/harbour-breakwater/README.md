# A breakwater, a harbour mouth, and a wood with trees in it

Round 8 of the badass harbour.

## What changed

- **THE BREAKWATER**: a stone mole runs from the cove's beach out round the bay, leaving the harbour
  mouth open to the south-west - the way the Seagallop comes in. It makes the bay a harbour rather
  than the edge of the sea, shelters the cove into an inner basin, and can be walked to its head;
  the part of the harbour has its own name (`harbourPlaces.ts`) and sign.
- **The harbour light stands at the breakwater's head** now - Brock's beacon rung, moved from the
  corner of the quay to where a harbour light goes - and at night its beam sweeps the bay from
  there.
- **The north woodline** was a ruled band of round bushes; big trees now stand in it at uneven
  spaces, so it reads as the edge of a wood.
- Every walk unchanged (yard: Arena 9, Center 5, Lab 11, Workshop 17, Bill 14, Bolthole 24; boat:
  Arena 11, Bill 4).

## How to use it

Walk east past the warehouses to the cove and out along the breakwater to its head; with the
beacon built, go at night.
