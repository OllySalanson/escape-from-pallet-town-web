# THE RAID ARENA's telescopes, its rim and its calm camera

Everything here is from `tools/playtest/arenaTelescope.mjs` against the dev server, at 1200x768 unless a name says
`small` (640x480). `before-` pictures are the build before the view was reworked as a Town Map.

**The walks and the camera**

- `before-east-wall.png`, `after-east-wall.png`: walking to the east wall. The camera used to turn round in the last
  few tiles, a swing of up to 19 pixels a frame, and showed black past the seats. Now it only leans in over the pit.
- `before-east-rail.png`, `after-east-rail.png`: the east walk, five tiles to the wall before and eight now, with the
  telescope on the rail.
- `at-the-telescope.png`: what a player meets. The name comes up from a few tiles off, and the plate in the floor is
  where to stand.
- `eye-to-the-glass.png`: the round of the glass closing on them, before it opens on the map.

**Through the glass**

- `before-whole-vale.png`, `whole-vale.png`: the map between black bars under drifting mist and a compass whose W, A, S
  and D sat next to its N, E, S and W; now the map on a sky to the screen's edges, the cloud calmed so walked ground is
  what reads, and a card beside the way in under the cursor.
- `before-closest.png`, `closest.png`: a closer look, with the whole map small in the corner saying where it is.
- `cursor-over-the-ground.png`: a key moves the cursor from the first press, and walked ground says its name.
- `no-way-in-here.png`: ENTER away from a way in, and the key dimmed along the foot.
- `pointing-at-a-way-in.png`: the mouse is the cursor. Only the way in under it says anything.
- `stopped-at-the-west-edge.png`: the view goes with the cursor and stops at the map, with the cursor whole.
- `whole-vale-wings.png`: something very large crossing it, now and then.
- `night-whole-vale.png`: the player's own clock, with walked ground lifted so it still reads.
- `before-new-save.png`, `new-save-whole-vale.png`: a save that has walked nothing: one way in and all cloud.
- `before-small-whole-vale.png`, `small-whole-vale.png`, `small-closest.png`: the smallest stage.

**The rim**

- `before-rim-panel.png`, `rim-card.png`, `small-rim-card.png`: two scrolling lists and `112 MORE`; now one card, as tall
  as what it says, with the map above it. SPACE opens it and ENTER jumps, as ever.
- `rim-another-way-in.png`: the arrows, or a click on a light, point at another way in on the map.

What used to scroll on the rim (what lives where, the ways out, the doors, what a map is worth) is read on Oak's wall
map, close up.
