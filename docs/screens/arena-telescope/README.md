# THE RAID ARENA's telescopes, its wider walks and its calm camera

Everything here is from `tools/playtest/arenaTelescope.mjs` against the dev server, at 1200x768 unless a name says
`small` (640x480), except the two `before-` pictures, which are the same walk on the build before this change.

- `before-east-wall.png`, `after-east-wall.png`: walking to the east wall. The camera used to turn round in the last
  few tiles, a swing of up to 19 pixels a frame, and showed black past the seats. Now it only leans in over the pit.
- `before-east-rail.png`, `after-east-rail.png`: the east walk, five tiles to the wall before and eight now, with the
  telescope on the rail.
- `at-the-telescope.png`: what a player meets. The name comes up from a few tiles off, and the plate in the floor is
  where to stand.
- `eye-to-the-glass.png`: the round of the glass closing on them, before it opens on the map.
- `whole-vale.png`, `whole-vale-wings.png`: the first thing it shows, all of Greystone Vale, with cloud, marks, a raid's
  walk and every way in, and something very large crossing it.
- `closest.png`: Z or the wheel, twice. Arrows, W A S D or a drag pan it, and it stops at the map's edge
  (`stopped-at-the-west-edge.png`).
- `next-way-in.png`, `clicked-a-way-in.png`: Q and E walk the ways in, and a click on a light chooses it.
- `looked-over-from-the-glass.png`: ENTER walks round to the rail and opens the rim's panel on that way in.
- `small-whole-vale.png`, `small-closest.png`, `small-east-wall.png`: the smallest stage.
- `new-save-whole-vale.png`: a save that has walked nothing.
