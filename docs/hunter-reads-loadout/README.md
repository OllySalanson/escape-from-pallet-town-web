# The hunter reads your loadout

Frames from three real raids on the Floodplain Relay, played end to end in
headless Chrome at 1280x657 against one save: a level-16 Charmander, three
level-6 Pidgeys and a level-5 Bulbasaur in the stash.

| file | what it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `final-check-strong.png` | The veteran packed alone. The confirm bar prices it before the button that pays it: hunter tier 3 of 3, a Lv 12 team of 3, arriving 30s sooner, matched to the Lv 16 Charmander. |
| `hunter-strong.png` | That raid, 54 seconds in. The hunter opens on a **Lv 12** Pidgey. |
| `hunter-strong-second.png` | The same fight after the Pidgey went down: a Lv 12 Bulbasaur behind it - the tier-3 team, not a relabelled tier 1. The veteran did not come home. |
| `final-check-weak.png` | The three Pidgeys packed instead, from the same save. Tier 1 of 3, and the at-risk list scrolls under the bar rather than pushing it off the frame. |
| `hunter-weak.png` | That raid. The hunter opens on a **Lv 6** Pidgey. |
| `final-check-recovering.png` | A player down to one Pokemon, the level-5 starter a wipe hands back. Tier 1 of 3. |
| `hunter-recovering.png` | That raid. Lv 6 Pidgey: a recovering player is on the tutorial hunter with no special case. |
| `raid1-start.png`, `raid2-start.png` | The opening view of two consecutive raids on the same map. The crate east of the ranger station in the first is not there in the second - each raid draws which of the map's loot is live and where it lies. |
| `extraction-report.png` | The second raid extracted through the Radio Exit carrying the Flooded Supply Vault. The stash went from 5 Poke Balls + 3 Potions to the same plus 2 Great Balls + 1 Super Potion: what was carried out, and nothing from a restock. |

The wipe in the first raid left that stash at 4 Poke Balls and 1 Potion - short
of the kit - and the last-resort restock topped it back to 5 and 3.
