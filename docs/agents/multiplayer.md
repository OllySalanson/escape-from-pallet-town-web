# Multiplayer

The shared harbour, quick phrases, the twelve regulars and raiding with a friend.

Moved out of `AGENTS.md` on 9 Nov 2026 so that file stays small; nothing here was reworded. Keep it current the way `AGENTS.md` asks: a change that adds a subsystem or makes a note here untrue updates the note in the same change, and a new note goes in the doc whose area it belongs to.

## Notes

- **THE HARBOUR is shared: everybody who plays walks the same one, live** (the captain's first multiplayer build, 2026-10-12; raids stay single-player). `src/game/harbour/` is all of it and `base/harbourFigures.ts` draws it. **What matters is the database's, what is drawn is the browsers'**: a player is *seen* only once their house is named at THE BOLTHOLE's door - `name_my_house` writes `public.player_houses` as the signed-in player through `maker/playerSignIn.ts` (the one sign-in, bot check and all; never write a second), on the console's own name rule and word filter, rate-limited - and only then may they speak on the private Realtime channel `harbour` (its insert policy asks `private.may_walk_the_harbour()`); anybody may watch, signed out included, so a newcomer sees a busy harbour and signs nobody up. A message is untrusted input read through `harbourProtocol.ts` (a known place, at most eight whole tiles, a facing) and **never carries a name**: the name over a figure is `harbour_names` by id, asked once per player and forgotten when they leave the channel - so a player renamed at the console leaves and rejoins it (`HarbourLink.renamed`) for everybody else to ask again - and a figure the database has no name for is not drawn. The one thing a move carries beyond the walk is the kind of partner at the player's heel (`k`), believed only as a kind with walking art (`isPartnerSpecies`) and otherwise dropped while the steps are kept; `HarbourFigures` walks it on the tile its player last left (`HarbourFigure.behind`, `heelOf`), arriving where the scene's own partner could and never on it. Facing another player and pressing the interact key **waves** (`HarbourWave`, the `wave` event - the channel's policy limits topics, not events, so a new kind of message needs no migration): a hand goes up over the waver on every screen in the place for `WAVE_SHOWN_MS` (`NamedFigure.waving`), the one waved at hears it (`whenWavedAt`), and it carries no words; a name plate beside this player slides clear of their own column (`clearOfSelf`), because the chevron over their head is drawn over everything. Steps go out in batches every `HARBOUR_SEND_EVERY_MS` and **not at all while nobody else is there**, which is what keeps it inside the free plan's 2M messages a month; a newcomer is greeted with one "here I am" from each player present. Places are the yard, the four keepers' rooms (shared), and `house:<uid>` for a player's own house, so two players at home are in two houses. `HarbourSession` (`harbour()`) lives for the page, across every `BaseScene` restart; a scene gone two seconds untracks, a minute leaves the channel - timed by `HarbourSession.away`, which keeps the link's clock going itself, because the scene that ticked it is the one that has gone (playtest 49: a player in a loadout or a raid stood on the doorstep for everybody until they came back). Other players are not collision, and are drawn a hair behind this player and their partner on the same row (`OTHER_PLAYERS_BEHIND`), because everybody lands on the same spawn and heel tiles and level with the partner they hid it whole. **A test never reaches the live harbour**: test mode turns it off unless the URL asks `harbour=local`, a `BroadcastChannel` stand-in between tabs of one browser, which `tools/playtest/sharedHarbour.mjs` plays with two tabs (`docs/screens/shared-harbour/`). Turning off *Allow public access* in the project's Realtime settings is optional owner hardening; nothing else in the game uses Realtime. **Knocking** (`home/knocking.ts`, `ui/KnockOverlay.ts`, `harbour/houseService.ts`, `harbour/houseDoor.ts`) is THE BOLTHOLE's garden sign (`HOME_SIGN.knocks`, the captain, 23 Oct - there is no knock key): read like any sign, it asks whose door in the ordinary text box and, once that is read (`BaseScene.say`'s `then`), lists every player with a house (`harbour_houses`, at home first); a knock is `knock_on` and is answered by its owner only (`knocks_at_my_door`, `answer_knock`, a ChoicePrompt with the cursor on NOT NOW), and a yes lets the knocker `visit_house` for ten minutes. Everything a knock depends on is the database's; the one thing a browser tells another is a `nudge` (look now), which costs one question if it is false - or, carrying the knock's id, that the knocker stopped, which only takes the owner's question down (`HouseDoor.withdrawn`); a question whose knock is no longer at the door, withdrawn or run out, is taken down with "X has gone from the step." The owner's door says they are home on the way in and every 30 s (home for 90 s after the last), with a **snapshot** of the house that is a save file of only what THE BOLTHOLE is drawn from (`home/houseSnapshot.ts`: furniture, its found pieces, badges, pennants, dolls, the starter - no team, items or money), capped at `HOUSE_SNAPSHOT_MAX_BYTES`, and the visitor reads it back through `deserializeGame`, the loader every save goes through, so somebody else's house is drawn and never believed. A visit is `BaseSceneData.visit`: the owner's house, their name on their things (`visitorThingName`), nothing of theirs used or moved (no decorating, no bed, PC or console), the stairs kept, and down off the mat home to the visitor's own step. `tools/playtest/knocking.mjs` plays all of it in two tabs over the local stand-in (`docs/screens/knocking/`).
- **THE HARBOUR talks in quick phrases, never typed words** (the captain, 29 Oct: strangers, maybe children, so nothing to moderate). `harbour/harbourPhrases.ts` is the list (a dozen, by *id*, so a build that does not know an id drops it rather than showing another phrase) and the pace held at both ends (`PhrasePace`: one every `SAY_EVERY_MS`, `SAY_BURST` in `SAY_BURST_MS`, a refused phrase never resets it); a phrase rides the harbour channel as its own `say` event (`HarbourSay`, `readHarbourSay` - the channel's policy limits topics, not events, so no migration), and `HarbourLink.figuresIn` carries the words as `NamedFigure.saying`. T opens `ui/PhrasePicker.ts` (FireRed's start menu in two columns: arrows, SPACE says, T or ESC shuts) and `base/speechBubble.ts` draws a bubble over the speaker's head at the emotes' depth in place of their name (the captain, 30 Oct), this player's own over their chevron; no name is ever drawn under a bubble (`HarbourFigures.clearNamesUnder`), and a bubble that meets another is seated beside it with its tail still on its speaker, or lifted (`bubbleSeat`). `base/harbourTalk.ts` is the page-life local side: this player's own phrase and the regulars' answers - now and then the nearest within `REGULAR_ANSWERS_WITHIN` says one of `REGULAR_ANSWERS` back, on this screen only, as everything about a regular is. `tools/playtest/sharedHarbour.mjs` says one across two tabs.
- **THE HARBOUR has twelve regulars who walk it like players** (`base/harbourBots.ts`, the captain, 29 Oct: "make it look like the game is populated"): never on the wire, so nothing that counts or lists players - the channel, the knock list, `harbourFiguresShown` - sees one (`harbourRegularsShown` is theirs). **Everything is a pure function of the clock**: one arrives every `BOT_SLOT_MS`, how many stay is a width that grows by at most one a slot (`presentWidth`), arrivals and departures inside a slot are ordered to keep 2-6 in at every moment (`slotEvents`), so two players see the same regulars in the same places and test mode's stepped clock (`regularsClock`, from `TEST_CLOCK_ORIGIN_MS`) gets the same harbour every run; `?regulars=off` leaves them out. **They do things** (the captain, 30 Oct; `base/harbourActivities.ts`, its header is the design): a visit picks one of a list - `stroll`, `wander` (a quarter of visits mostly just potter), `hang-out`, `watch-sea`, `window-shop`, `read-board`, `knock`, `call-in`, `chat` - from its visit number alone, does it, picks again, and goes as it came, through a door or over the side of a boat (`BOT_GATES`, never THE BOLTHOLE's: that is each player's own home), fading through the step (`BotWhere.fade`), so nobody pops up in view. Where each thing is done and which way it faces is an authored table (`SEA_VIEWS`, `HANG_OUTS`, `SHOP_WINDOWS`, `NOTICE_BOARDS`, `CHAT_CIRCLES`), held by `harbourActivities.test.ts` to what stands there. A **chat** is an appointment read off the schedule (`chatInWindow`) so each member can be told without asking the others; a **call** is the room's for its window (`roomCaller`, `ROOM_CALLS`, drawn only while the player is in that room); a **knock** is a hand raised on a keeper's doorstep (`KNOCKED_DOORS`), and it reaches nobody. A visit's words and waves are part of its plan (`says`, `waves`), so they are the same on every screen, and rare. **Nobody walks through anybody, and that is a book rather than a reaction**: plans are made in visit order against `Bookings` (who holds which tile when), a walk goes round whoever stands about and gives way on the spot to whoever is passing - and because a plan that read the plan before it would have to be worked out from the start of time, the chain is cut in blocks of `PLAN_BLOCK_VISITS`: odd blocks stand alone, an even block plans round the odd one each side. What the book cannot clear is caught where they are drawn (`HarbourRegulars.stepsTowards`, `HarbourCrowd.advance`'s `mayStep`): a step onto a tile the player or somebody else is on is put off or walked round, and the figure catches the plan up, which is also all that keeps a regular off the player. A loiter spot is open ground off every lane, door, mat, the spawn, the landing, signs, beats and the sea; they are generated (`tools/base/botSpots.mts` writes `generated/harbourBotSpots.ts`, and `harbourBots.test.ts` fails until a yard change re-runs it), because searching them costs a page a hundred milliseconds. `harbourRegulars.ts` walks them through `HarbourCrowd` exactly as another player is walked and draws them with `HarbourFigures` - and tells the crowd only steps it has not heard (`HarbourCrowd.headingTo`), as a player's browser sends them: told the step they were on every frame, the crowd queued the tile just left as a step back and walked them to and fro at catch-up pace (the captain, 29 Oct; `tools/playtest/regularsWalk.mjs` samples every drawn frame). Their names are handles a player would type, held to `checkPlayerName`. A regular or another player never costs a door its name: figures are soft to a caption (`follower`), which reads through them when it must (`HarbourFigures.veilUnder`), and only their bubbles are kept clear. **The interact key does nothing at a regular** - no box, no hand (a real player faced is waved at, `BaseScene.waveAt`) - though one standing about turns to a player who comes near and waves once a visit, and one may answer a phrase (`base/harbourTalk.ts`). `tools/playtest/regularsActivities.mjs` is the long stepped watch: no two on a tile, no bubble over a name, every activity seen, the key doing nothing; `harbourRegulars.mjs` the count and a call on a keeper.
- **Raiding with a friend is a room by code, on the harbour's own link** (multiplayer phase 1, 13 Oct; plan: rooms of 2-4, own clocks, one hunter for the group, first-step loot, phrases and a ping, shared discoveries, boss doors for all - landed in stages). `src/game/raidRoom/` is all of it: the database decides who made a room, its map and who is in it (`*_raid_rooms*.sql`: `open_raid_room`, `join_raid_room`, `leave_raid_room`, four consonants a code, four a room, one hour open, every count under a lock), and only members of an open room may listen or speak on its private channel `raid:<code>` (`may_walk_raid_room`). The channel is `HarbourLink<RaidPlace>` over a raid wire - `HarbourLink`, `HarbourCrowd` and `HarbourWire` are generic over the place type, so a new shared space is a protocol and a wire, never a second link - and `FriendRoom` (`friendRoom()`, kept for the page) is the session every scene asks. A room is made or joined in Oak's Lab (`HubScene` view `friend`, markup `hub/friendRoomView.ts`), and while one is held the drop-in is held to its map and `startRun` refuses any other; `WorldScene.joinFriendRoom`/`updateFriend` draw the others with `HarbourFigures` and the friend chip (`friendChipLines`), and `ExtractionScene` leaves the room on every raid ending. **One hunter for the room** (`raidRoom/groupHunter.ts`): the browser with the lowest id on the map and not in a fight is its *keeper* and runs it as a raid alone does - quarry the nearest player not in a fight, a step only when that quarry steps - and says it on the room's `signal` event (`HarbourWire.signal`, `HarbourLink.whenSignalled`); everybody else draws it there (`WorldScene.huntRole`, `takeSharedHunter`) and runs none of their own. A catch is the caught player's own fight; it is said as `busy`, the hunter holds and the keeper falls silent until the fighter's `after` (fled and where to, or beaten for all), which the keeper takes over. A keeper that leaves or starts a fight hands over by the next in line taking the last word heard. **Loot to whoever steps on it first** (`raidRoom/roomLoot.ts`): the room map's loot is laid from the room's database seed (`generateRoomLoot`: every gate shut, from the front door, on its own stream, so it is the same ground in every member's raid and moves nothing else) and swapped into the plan at deploy (`withRoomLoot`, `RunPlan.roomLootIds`; an errand's thing stays the player's own). A step onto a shared piece is a claim (`take_raid_loot`, first claim holds under a lock); the piece is collected on the frame the answer is this player's (`WorldScene.settleRoomLoot`), and a winner's `took` signal and `raid_loot_taken` on drop-in take it off everybody else's map. **Quick phrases and a ping** (`raidRoom/roomTalk.ts`, keys in `ROOM_KEY_LETTERS`): T opens six fixed phrases, 1-6 says one, G pings the tile stood on; a phrase is an index, never text, and talk is paced at both ends (`SAY_EVERY_MS`, `PING_EVERY_MS`). A friend's name plate is drawn at `RAID_NAME_PLATE_DEPTH`, above the canopy. **Shared discoveries and boss doors**: a friend's walk is surveyed into this raid's own survey (`WorldScene.surveyWithFriends`), written at the raid's end as ever; a boss win is said to the room (`raidRoom/roomBosses.ts`, `FriendRoom.won`, repeated to anybody who arrives) and opens that keeper's doors - and stands the keeper aside - in every member's raid for that raid only (`WorldScene.roomDoors`, read by `openedDoorKeys` and `trainersForCurrentMap`, rebuilt in place by `openRoomDoors`), never in their save. Nothing in a message is believed beyond where it draws something. Tests use the harbour's switch: `?harbour=local` puts rooms in `localStorage` and the wire on a `BroadcastChannel`, and `tools/playtest/friendRaid.mjs` plays two tabs (`docs/screens/raid-with-a-friend/`); the two tabs share one save, so the second steps round the first's raid-in-flight mark, which no pair of devices needs.
