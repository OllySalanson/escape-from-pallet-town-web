# THE RAID ARENA

The great hall where every raid is got ready for and started: stations, the rim, the telescope, the jump, the crowd, the pit and the trailer.

Moved out of `AGENTS.md` on 9 Nov 2026 so that file stays small; nothing here was reworded. Keep it current the way `AGENTS.md` asks: a change that adds a subsystem or makes a note here untrue updates the note in the same change, and a new note goes in the doc whose area it belongs to.

## Notes

- **THE RAID ARENA's great hall is the room behind its door, and every raid is got ready for and started on its floor** (the captain, 30 Oct 2026: "the menu screens felt disengaging"; `base/arenaHall.ts` is the geometry and its header the promises, `docs/screens/arena-hall/`, `docs/screens/arena-raid-start/`): an oval more than four times the width of any other room, its pit shrunk and its ring widened on 1 Nov 2026 (`PIT_RIM`: thirteen tiles of floor east and west, ten north and south), walked with the camera following and leaning in over the pit (`cameraLean`, only ever inward and a share of a step for every step, so walking never swings it; up against the fence, from any side, `railLean` takes it right out over the pit with the player just inside the screen's edge, each axis leaned on its own so a curve of the fence puts them in a corner, and it eases back as they step away - the captain, 26 Nov 2026: "the edge of the screen is at the player and as much of the view as possible is over the portal"; `cameraEased` is the one way it goes anywhere else, capped at 4 pixels a frame), a platform all round and wider east and west than north and south, and a pit in the middle whose floor is the map a raid would go to from high up - the wall map's own picture a pixel to a tile (a smaller map at the most whole pixels that fit, `pitFrameFor`), cloud over what this save has not walked. **The hall is painted, not cut**, because FireRed has no curve: `scripts/draw-arena-hall.mts` paints it in FireRed's conventions (`STONE_RAMP`, ordered dither) and slices it into `arena-hall.png` (from 23000) and `base/generated/arenaHallTiles.ts`, so it is an ordinary room of tile layers - edit the script and `arenaHall.ts` and run it again, never the PNG. What is under those layers is painted at run time, Phaser-free (`base/arenaPit.ts`) and drawn by `base/arenaView.ts`: the floor, mist, a sheer wall with gates and four rings of steps, each its own picture sliding against the rest as the player walks (`parallaxShift`; the floor slides half as far as they walk, `FLOOR_PARALLAX`, which is what puts the map under them wherever they stand); `tools/base/renderArena.mts` draws it without a browser. **The whole map is seen through a telescope, worked as FireRed's Town Map is** (the captain, 31 Oct 2026; `base/valeOverview.ts` is the rules, `base/valeOverviewView.ts` draws it, `ARENA_TELESCOPES` stand at the two ends of the fence's straight, on the first screen through the great doors, with a brass plate on the rail's own row where a player stands): the interact key on the plate or facing it brings the eye to the glass (an iris on a timeline, a cut under reduced motion) onto the whole map, the cloud past its edges filling the sides so there are no black bars (`wholePicture`), and the cloud drawn down to a quiet grey-blue so walked ground is the only bright thing there (`calmedFloor`, `steppedCloud`: the pit's own floor, repainted per look because a raid changes it). **There is one thing to move and it always moves**: arrows, W A S D or the mouse move a cursor, on the whole map as closer up, and the view goes with it near the screen's edge; a drag moves the view, Z or the wheel go closer. A way in draws the cursor as it passes and holds it a moment, so a held key cannot overshoot, and **only what is under the cursor is said**: a small card beside it (`cardPlace`, `base/wayInCard.ts`: a clause of its own words and, only if walked, what lives there), or the name of walked ground, or the line a raid left there (`placeAt`, marks), never anything under the cloud. ENTER or a click goes round to the rail and opens the rim's card on it. Q and E hop the ways in, the player's head stands on the one they are stood over, little houses mark towns walked (`townsOf`), and the corners hold a compass, how much is walked and, closer up, the whole map small with what is in sight outlined. Everything in it stands on the screen's own coordinates over the hall, so the hall's camera has nothing to do with it. `tools/playtest/arenaTelescope.mjs` plays it, and the camera round the ring, with keys and mouse; `docs/screens/arena-telescope/`. **Getting ready is walking, up a tunnel**: the door opens into a tunnel under the south stands (the captain, 25 Nov 2026; `isTunnel`, `TUNNEL_BOXES`, `docs/screens/arena-hallway/`), all of it the hall's own warm stone, dark wall tops and torchlit flags (the old room's pale flags and blue wall did not fit the colosseum), a corridor between two torch pillars up from the mat to a room at its end, and the bench (who comes), the armoury rack (what is packed, how, and which pack) and the locker (the secure container) are `ARENA_STATIONS` spaced along that room's back wall with a standing torch between each, named on plates hung from a brass rail in the Arena's own lettering (`STATION_PLATE_RISE`, which their captions clear), off every way anywhere, painted wholly on the floor layer because nothing can pass behind them and what stands on them rises into the row above, `RoomThing`s that open a panel when faced, and what was chosen stands on them (`base/arenaFurniture.ts`; the partner is at the player's heel, so never on the bench too). **Between them stand the great doors into the oval** (`ARENA_DOOR`, `DOOR_OPENING`; `base/arenaDoors.ts` is the timeline, the leaves' pictures, their light and the welcome, `arenaDoorsView.ts` puts them on screen): every tile of the way through is floor to the hall's collision, and the scene holds them shut until they are pushed, by walking into them (the step waits, and the held key walks on through once they are back) or SPACE at them, so every driver's path through `isBlocked` still finds the rim and only has to keep pressing. They swing on the scene clock with the `arenaDoors` creak, the hall's light fans out through them, and as they come to rest the house greets whoever comes through as much as their record has filled it (`doorsWelcome`: hands, a cheer, a roar). They stay open from the push until the player walks out by the mat (`hallDoorsOpen` in `BaseScene`), so a raid, a bout or a panel coming back finds them open; only coming in from the harbour finds them shut. The wall's top over them is painted over figures, so the way through is walked under. `tools/playtest/arenaDoors.mjs` plays and photographs them. **The stations are the harbour's own screens on the hall's choices**: the bench is YOUR TEAM and the rack and the locker are YOUR THINGS (`BaseScene.openHallPanel`; the pack flow of 26 Nov 2026, `docs/screens/pack-flow/`), each handed the visit's `kit.flow`, so what is chosen there is what the rim deploys, and closing one stands what was packed on the rack and the pack on the player's back. `HubScene` has no bench, rack or locker view any more. **A panel is `HubScene` launched over the hall** with `view` `rim` (once also `bench`, `rack` and `locker`) and an `ArenaKit` (`base/arenaKit.ts`: the one `DeploymentFlow` for the visit, re-based on the vault each time a panel opens, and how to `close`, `jump` and `aim`), docked to whole game pixels down the right of the window - the rim's too wherever the window has room for the portal beside it, else along its foot (`dockPanel`, `data-rim-side`), so the hall keeps burning beside it - `BaseScene` is not paused, only `arenaPanel` stops its walking, and `settleHallCamera` keeps the player in what the panel leaves. Escape is back on the same tile. **Choosing a raid is walking the rim** (`base/arenaRim.ts`): the ways in are read round the map from its middle and the rim shared out among them an equal stretch each, turned to lie over where they are, so every way in has a stretch of its own even in the middle of the map and with one the whole rim is over it (`arenaRim.test.ts` holds that each shows through the hole from most of its stretch). Anywhere on the rim SPACE looks down. **Looking down is the portal: the pit seen whole** (the captain, 25 Nov 2026: "the camera kind of hovers half over the arena floor and half over the map, and you can't really see that it's like a whole portal"; `base/arenaPortal.ts` is the rules and timelines, `base/arenaPortalView.ts` draws it, `docs/screens/arena-portal/`): the pit is far bigger than the screen, so the view draws back from the rail (the pit under the rail shrinking into place, `portalOpenPose`) to one round picture of all of it - kerb, fence posts, steps, the dark wall and the floor under its cloud (`condensedFloor`, the pit's own floor drawn down), the ways in as lights and the player and partner stood on its edge where they stand round the rim (`portalStance`) - in the room the card leaves (`portalShapeFor`; the hall goes all but dark round it and its own figures are hidden, so nobody is seen twice). Choosing another way in hops the look mark over to its light (`aimHopAt`), its name on a plate that keeps off the other lights, and a click on a light chooses it (`lightsOnScreen`). ESC plays the drawing back in reverse. A window with no room for one beside or above the card (a phone, an 800x512 window) keeps the old look round to the way in, and still jumps down into the portal. The rim's card (`HubScene.rimView`) has what the raid risks and JUMP IN, with **no list and nothing to scroll**, drawn as pictures and numbers (`hub/rimCard.ts`, the captain, 25 Nov 2026: "far too many words"; `docs/screens/jump-in-card/`): on the left three `< VALUE >` rows - LAND, JOB, SKY - the clock they leave and the buttons, on the right four tiles (who comes and who is padlocked safe, what a wipe takes, who hunts and how hard, what the job pays against its walk), and every sentence the card used to print is the help line under whatever is pointed at or highlighted, which is two lines deep whatever it says so the card never changes height. **One key, one job** (`hub/rimCard.ts`; the captain, 25 and 26 Nov 2026): the arrows only ever move between the drop-ins, by where they lie on the map (`rimLandingToward`: the nearest light that way on the same map, a step to the side counting double; nothing that way is a bump), with the pit's reticle and name plate as the highlight; **Enter jumps in** (`HubScene.rimEnter`; a card that opened away from JUMP IN - a hurt team, nobody able to go, a player just home - takes a first Enter to put the highlight on JUMP IN, so a press meant for the result screen never spends the raid); Tab and Shift+Tab take the highlight round the rows and JUMP IN (`rimTabStep`), and SPACE on JOB or SKY steps it on (on LAND it is a bump, so SPACE mashed through the result never moves the landing). A click on a light (`pointAtLanding`) or on a row's arrows still works; the arrows on the LAND row go map by map and west to east (`rimLandingOrder`). The arrows once pointed at the map and fell back to the buttons, so one key did two jobs by where the lights lay, and then walked the rows, so a landing was three presses up and a walk west to east; `tools/playtest/rimCard.mjs` plays the keys and fails on any key doing two jobs. The cursor still opens on JUMP IN, so SPACE, ENTER is all it takes. It opens with nobody on the bench too, and then JUMP IN says why not (`DeploymentFlow.notReady`), though walking into the hall puts somebody there when nobody was and somebody can fight (`packSomebodyIfBenchEmpty`: the partner, else the strongest, one only). It names who a wipe would lose for good, and a badly hurt team is told so on the card (`hub/railWarning.ts`), which says what Nurse Joy would do for them, at her own price (`joyRailLine` in `hub/centerCounter.ts`: 'for 0:45 of your next raid', the day's free heal, or - with the next raid already as short as she makes it - that she heals again after this raid): with the whole team under a third of its HP, or nobody able to go, the cursor opens on the card's first row rather than JUMP IN - and so it does on every card opened home from a raid until a step is taken (`BaseScene.homeOnTheRail`, `HubSceneData.justHome`), because SPACE mashed through the result screen opened the card and jumped. The card has no NOT YET since 25 Nov 2026: its way back is its own X in its top corner (`.rim-close`, since the card shows no title bar) and ESC, as on every menu; a highlight lost by a click on a tile goes back to the first row, never to JUMP IN (`HubScene.rimArrow`). The player is never stopped from jumping, keyboard included: Down from the last row is always JUMP IN (`HubScene.handleKey`; only TAB, said nowhere, used to leave the card's safe start - the second characters hunt, 11 Nov 2026). Where the card opens is `rimOpensAwayFromJumpIn()` in `hub/railWarning.ts`. The floor's hint at the rail says `[SPACE] LOOK DOWN` from every rail, because that is what SPACE does there; the jump is a press on from the portal. A pointer works the room as well: a click on a station or in the pit walks there and opens it (`BaseScene.pointAtHall`, `walkThen`). **A jump is a pure timeline** (`base/arenaDive.ts`, `world/raidArrival.ts`, each `*Pose(elapsedMs)`): the held breath on the portal's edge (the portal comes to the screen's middle and the hall's camera moves with it, so the runner still meets the player; `base/heldBreath.ts`: the fires drop, nothing is heard, what would be lost stamps across the screen under AT RISK - all of it for a team's first raids, `raidProgress.benchRaids`, then only a Pokémon at risk or a pack better than the Raid pack, and with neither no hold at all - and any key cuts it short, no third press), the hop with a horn and fire running round the hall (`base/ringOfFire.ts`, on the way out only), a flash, the house on its feet and the floor shaking, then the player and partner falling at twice their size (`fallerScale`) with wind and torn cloud, the raid's hunter coming over the rail after them and going on down past them, seen and not heard, because `hunterArrival` is the one word for a hunter entering the raid (`pursuerAt`, read off the plan's own rival), the hop off the portal's edge and down into it, its steps and floor coming up together until its hole is wider than the screen (`portalDiveScales`, never past three times over), and the cloud (`cloudSheet`) shutting over it before `scene.start('world', { droppedIn: true })`, which opens under the same sheet and has them fall into a still picture with a thump and dust - free of the raid clock, as nothing runs but that (`WorldScene.advanceArrival`); nobody on the map reacts, because "!" means hunted; reduced motion is a plain fade. Whatever falls is on a camera of its own (`base/arenaDiveView.ts`), never zoomed: a magnified dither is a board of squares, and a Phaser camera added stays across scene restarts unless removed. **The rings and stands are drawn as only their inked squares** (`base/sparsePicture.ts`, a Blitter a picture): as whole images, eight mostly empty hall-sized pictures blended over every pixel of every frame, which with no graphics card halved the frame rate against the harbour's. A new big, mostly empty picture in the hall goes through `addSparsePicture`. **Nobody stands behind anything and one key does a side of the hall** (the captain, 1 Nov 2026: "I have to zigzag with the arrow keys the whole way"; `docs/screens/arena-flow/`). The pillars stand back against the wall with a gap at the great doors (and two more frame the tunnel's mouth), what rises over the floor is solid under the part that would hide a figure (a pillar's shaft, `ArenaStation.rises`), and floor with one way in is taken away unless it fronts a station (`WALLED_OFF`). `base/arenaWalk.ts` is the walk: a key held against a stair of the oval goes on round it (`slideRound`, asked only in the hall by `BaseScene.roundTheStair`), never round anything pressed into on purpose, and a tap from standing only turns. Up from the mat one key goes to the great doors and, once they are pushed, on to the rail; from the doors left and right go to the bench and the rack, and along the runner's arms in front of the stations to whichever is wanted; a torch at a station's end answers for it (`ArenaStation.past`, `RoomThing.alsoFaced`). `arenaWalk.test.ts` holds the routes and `tools/playtest/arenaFlow.mjs` counts them through the real scene: key changes, stops and tiles. The yard has one such stair of its own (`YARD_STAIR`), the foot of the Arena's east banner on the way every new player first walks. **All three tiles of the hall's mat lead out** (`ARENA_MAT_REACH`). **Round the hall stand the stands** (`base/arenaStands.ts`, painted at run time under the hall's layers, on negative coordinates, so the hall's grid and every tile fact are untouched): seats, a dim crowd of which a few shift by a pixel, an arcade and banners; nothing the camera can reach is left black but the way out: past the seats and the arcade `paintStands` lays the colosseum's roof to the picture's edge, which is the camera's bounds (`STANDS_WIDTH`/`STANDS_HEIGHT` follow the hall). **The pit is alive and keeps the player's clock**: a rare shadow and eyes in a gate (`base/pitLife.ts`, never explained anywhere in the game), and the floor and town lights by `windowLight` (`base/pitClock.ts`, lights only on walked ground). **Home is the same rim**: `homeAfterRaid` is `{ room: 'raid-arena', arrival: 'raid' }`, and the cloud over whatever the raid walked is laid back and thinned on a dither in the portal, glinting where it lifts (`ArenaPortalView.refreshFloor`), when the raid gained at least `FOG_LIFT_LEAST_GAIN` of the map, and otherwise clears unwatched (`ArenaView.liftFogFromLastRaid`, compared with the picture kept at the jump, so a reload just shows the ground). The press that cuts a lift short also does what it was pressed for (`advanceFogLift` says which), so home, SPACE, ENTER costs no extra press. The Seagallop ties up and sounds her horn the first time the player is next out in the yard (`boatDueHome`), as harbour life and nothing more. The haul is shown going into YOUR THINGS on the result screen, never on the quay (the captain, 25 Nov 2026: a pile of pictures that looked like loot and could not be picked up, signed for a "vault" nobody could find, confused him); the result screen alone says what was secured, risked and spent, and says it of a lost raid. `tools/playtest/arenaHall.mjs` plays all of it; `walkIntoBase(page, 'raid-arena')` opens the bench, `openHallPanel` and `jumpIn` in `tools/playtest/deploy.mjs` are the rest.
- **THE RAID ARENA's crowd answers the player** (`base/arenaCrowd.ts`, its header the design; `docs/screens/arena-fun/`, and `docs/screens/arena-tidy/` for what the last sweep pared back). Its noises and the wind of a fall are the sound channel `hall`, so a partner's chirp or a world sound cannot cut them. How full the stands are is worked out from `raidRecord` (`crowdShare`; `crowdSeats` is every place, `seatedAt(share)` who is in, so a fuller house is the same people with more beside them), so `arenaStands.ts` paints the seats, the people (`paintCrowdLayer`, repainted when the share changes) and the banners (over anybody on their feet) as separate pictures. The result screen hands the raid's ending to the hall as `BaseSceneData.raidMood` (`moodOfRaid`): the view looks round at the seats for `CROWD_LOOK_MS` on a roar or a groan only (`crowdTakesTheView`; a step or any panel takes it back, and a pending fog lift waits behind it; neither adds a press to home-SPACE-ENTER) and the crowd roars with paper, cheers, claps or groans with some of them leaving and the fires low. The interact key at the outer wall (`isOuterWall`) waves and the nearest section waves back; going a quarter of the way round the outer floor runs a band of people beside the player (`LapWatch`), with the view left where it is, and on the rim nobody gets up, because that is choosing a way in; a whole lap brings the house up and the board says the time. `tools/playtest/arenaFun.mjs` plays it.
- **The hall keeps how close a raid was.** It used to keep the run too, a slate on the runner chalked `2 OUT` for raids out in a row, until the captain could not read it (26 Nov 2026: "I have no idea what two out means"); it bought nothing, so it went, with the save's count behind it. A raid out with the clock red or a hunter on its tail (`closeCallLine`, `world/raidMemory.ts`) gets the crowd to its feet whatever it hauled, says so on the result screen and the hall's board, and writes it as the flag's line in the pit. It buys nothing.
- **A landing out of the pit can be stuck** (`world/raidArrival.ts`): a ring closes on the tile and the interact key as it shuts (`isCleanLanding`, one press a landing) gets a sharper thump, wider dust, no crouch, a bigger bounce from the partner and a board over the player's head. It buys nothing, moves no time, and a miss is the landing as it always was. `raidProgress.landings` (`SaveManager.recordLanding`, optional, no version bump) is only for pride: the board and the marshal. A stepped driver presses a frame early (`arenaFun.mjs`): a 100ms frame can step over the window. The arrow keys drift whoever is falling (`steered`), for the feel of it.
- **The pit remembers the raids that went down into it** (`world/raidMemory.ts`; `raidProgress.raidMemory`, optional, no version bump): a cross or a flag where each of the last dozen ended, faded by age, and the last raid's walk as a dotted line, drawn on the floor's own layer by `ArenaView.drawMemory`. The walk rides on `ActiveRunSession.walk`, outdoors only, and goes home with `recordRaidEnded` from each ending; a mark's line is written then and never worked out again, because the Pokemon it names may be long gone. At the rail the rim names the mark in view nearest the player (`nameTheMark`). A map is smaller than the hole, so from one rail only the near half of it is on the screen: a rule about what is looked at asks what is in view, not `pitLookPoint`.
- **A coin can go over the rail, and nothing explains it** (`base/pitCoins.ts`, key C at the rim, named only until first used): ₽10 out of the vault, a long wait, a plink; eyes in a gate look at whoever threw it. After more and more coins (`coinsForGift`) something comes up out of the pit and lies by the runner until walked on to (`raidProgress.pit`: coins and how many picked up, optional, no version bump, so what is due is derived). `pitCoins.test.ts` holds every gift to cosmetic and humble - common furniture or an everyday hat, never a prize - and dearer in coins than in the shop.
- **Bouts for show: the next hunter stands on the hall's far floor and will fight for nothing** (`base/arenaBouts.ts`, `base/boutLadder.ts`, each rival's `hall` lines in `world/hunters.ts`). **A bout can never write to the save, by construction**: the real `BattleScene` with `exhibition` set and no run session (`create` refuses both), on deep copies of the bench through the save's own serialiser (`boutParty`), a copy of the rack and no experience, and the only code in that scene that touches a save is `resolveRunWipe`, behind a run session - `scenes/exhibitionBout.test.ts` fails a second place that names one, and `tools/playtest/arenaFun.mjs --only=bout` compares the save byte for byte. A new thing a fight writes must stay behind `runSession`. **Everybody on the far floor has one tile, every day**: a gym leader the player has met stands on their own (`LEADER_TILES`, by gym order) and the rival always under the banner (`RIVAL_TILE`); no hunter is a gym leader (since 22 Nov 2026), so nobody stands there twice. Ask `rivalTile`, never `RIVAL_TILE`, for where the rival is. All that goes home is which way it went (`BaseSceneData.bout`), and the hall writes the one field, `raidProgress.bouts` (optional, no version bump): who has been beaten in a row, and how often all five have been, which hangs the player's banner. Walking up turns the view to the wall and the crowd (`nearTheRival`); YIELD is a loss, so a bout with something to lose (the ladder here; a run of wins, a cup or rental run, or the day's paying rental run in THE BATTLE ARENA) asks "Yield the bout?" on NO first, with the room's words for the stake on `HallBout`/`DojoBout.stake` (`arena/yieldStake.ts`): it sits one press right of FIGHT. A question in the box must fit one line or it lies over its answers, so a rival's offer is said first and `BOUT_QUESTION` asked after.
- **THE RAID ARENA's door plays a trailer before its hall, once, the first time through it after a first raid** (the captain, 2026-10-29: "when I walk into the gym for the first time ... an epic cutscene of me in a raid"; held back past the first raid on 1 Nov 2026, when he asked for fewer barriers to one): `cutscene/arenaIntro.ts` is the script and its rules (cast, every word, beat times), `cutscene/arenaIntroForest.ts` the wood it is set in, `scenes/ArenaIntroScene.ts` plays it with the game's own art - the player in their own look (`lookTexture`, as the harbour draws them) and their name, their partner in the fight, their next raid's hunter (`rivalForRaid`) and that hunter's team, a comic panel for Charizard. Any key or click past `ARENA_INTRO_ARMING_MS` ends it in the great hall, where the door goes. Who says the closing line is `ARENA_INTRO_HOST` (the Gym Guide until the captain says otherwise). **Its motion runs on the scene clock (`move`), never `this.tweens`**: Phaser's tween manager keeps the wall clock, so under a stepped loop or a slow frame every tween drifts off the `this.time` cues it was choreographed against. Big words are `ui/stampedLettering.ts`, the title logo's own stamping. `walkIntoBase` presses through it; `tools/playtest/arenaIntro.mjs` films every beat and checks once, skip and the loadout.
