# THE RAID ARENA

The great hall where every raid is got ready for and started: stations, the rim, the telescope, the jump, the crowd, the pit and the trailer.

Moved out of `AGENTS.md` on 9 Nov 2026 so that file stays small; nothing here was reworded. Keep it current the way `AGENTS.md` asks: a change that adds a subsystem or makes a note here untrue updates the note in the same change, and a new note goes in the doc whose area it belongs to.

## Notes

- **THE RAID ARENA's great hall is the room behind its door, and every raid is got ready for and started on its floor** (the captain, 30 Oct 2026: "the menu screens felt disengaging"; `base/arenaHall.ts` is the geometry and its header the promises, `docs/screens/arena-hall/`, `docs/screens/arena-raid-start/`): an oval more than four times the width of any other room, its pit shrunk and its ring widened on 1 Nov 2026 (`PIT_RIM`: thirteen tiles of floor east and west, ten north and south), walked with the camera following and leaning in over the pit (`cameraLean`, only ever inward and a share of a step for every step, so walking never swings it; `cameraEased` is the one way it goes anywhere else, capped at 4 pixels a frame), a platform all round and wider east and west than north and south, and a pit in the middle whose floor is the map a raid would go to from high up - the wall map's own picture a pixel to a tile (a smaller map at the most whole pixels that fit, `pitFrameFor`), cloud over what this save has not walked. **The hall is painted, not cut**, because FireRed has no curve: `scripts/draw-arena-hall.mts` paints it in FireRed's conventions (`STONE_RAMP`, ordered dither) and slices it into `arena-hall.png` (from 23000) and `base/generated/arenaHallTiles.ts`, so it is an ordinary room of tile layers - edit the script and `arenaHall.ts` and run it again, never the PNG. What is under those layers is painted at run time, Phaser-free (`base/arenaPit.ts`) and drawn by `base/arenaView.ts`: the floor, mist, a sheer wall with gates and four rings of steps, each its own picture sliding against the rest as the player walks (`parallaxShift`; the floor slides half as far as they walk, `FLOOR_PARALLAX`, which is what puts the map under them wherever they stand); `tools/base/renderArena.mts` draws it without a browser. **The whole map is seen through a telescope, worked as FireRed's Town Map is** (the captain, 31 Oct 2026; `base/valeOverview.ts` is the rules, `base/valeOverviewView.ts` draws it, `ARENA_TELESCOPES` stand at the two ends of the fence's straight, on the first screen from the door, with a brass plate on the rail's own row where a player stands): the interact key on the plate or facing it brings the eye to the glass (an iris on a timeline, a cut under reduced motion) onto the whole map, the cloud past its edges filling the sides so there are no black bars (`wholePicture`), and the cloud drawn down to a quiet grey-blue so walked ground is the only bright thing there (`calmedFloor`, `steppedCloud`: the pit's own floor, repainted per look because a raid changes it). **There is one thing to move and it always moves**: arrows, W A S D or the mouse move a cursor, on the whole map as closer up, and the view goes with it near the screen's edge; a drag moves the view, Z or the wheel go closer. A way in draws the cursor as it passes and holds it a moment, so a held key cannot overshoot, and **only what is under the cursor is said**: a small card beside it (`cardPlace`, `base/wayInCard.ts`: a clause of its own words and, only if walked, what lives there), or the name of walked ground, or the line a raid left there (`placeAt`, marks), never anything under the cloud. ENTER or a click goes round to the rail and opens the rim's card on it. Q and E hop the ways in, the player's head stands on the one they are stood over, little houses mark towns walked (`townsOf`), and the corners hold a compass, how much is walked and, closer up, the whole map small with what is in sight outlined. Everything in it stands on the screen's own coordinates over the hall, so the hall's camera has nothing to do with it. `tools/playtest/arenaTelescope.mjs` plays it, and the camera round the ring, with keys and mouse; `docs/screens/arena-telescope/`. **Getting ready is walking**: the door opens into a squared-off room of pale flags under a deep blue wall (`ARENA_ENTRY`, the one place in the hall not its warm stone, because stone furniture on it could not be seen), and the bench (who comes), the armoury rack (what is packed, how, and which pack) and the locker (the secure container) are `ARENA_STATIONS` in its corners, named on plates over them in the Arena's own lettering (`STATION_PLATE_RISE`, which their captions clear), off every way anywhere, `RoomThing`s that open a panel when faced, and what was chosen stands on them (`base/arenaFurniture.ts`; the partner is at the player's heel, so never on the bench too). **A panel is `HubScene` launched over the hall** with `view` `bench`, `rack`, `locker` or `rim` and an `ArenaKit` (`base/arenaKit.ts`: the one `DeploymentFlow` for the visit, re-based on the vault each time a panel opens, and how to `close`, `jump` and `aim`), docked to whole game pixels down the right of the window, or along its foot for the rim (`dockPanel`), so the hall keeps burning beside it - `BaseScene` is not paused, only `arenaPanel` stops its walking, and `settleHallCamera` keeps the player in what the panel leaves. Escape is back on the same tile. **Choosing a raid is walking the rim** (`base/arenaRim.ts`): the ways in are read round the map from its middle and the rim shared out among them an equal stretch each, turned to lie over where they are, so every way in has a stretch of its own even in the middle of the map and with one the whole rim is over it (`arenaRim.test.ts` holds that each shows through the hole from most of its stretch). Anywhere on the rim SPACE looks down: the rim's card (`HubScene.rimView`) swings the camera to the way in with what the raid risks and JUMP IN, as tall as what it says, with **no list and nothing to scroll**: the arrows and a click on a light point at another way in on the map (`wayInToward`, `pointAtLanding`; the cursor stays on JUMP IN, so SPACE, ENTER is still all it takes), and a button puts another map in the pit when there is one (`ArenaView.showMap`). It opens with nobody on the bench too, and then JUMP IN says why not (`DeploymentFlow.notReady`), though walking into the hall puts somebody there when nobody was and somebody can fight (`packSomebodyIfBenchEmpty`: the partner, else the strongest, one only). It names who a wipe would lose for good, and a badly hurt team is told so on the card (`hub/railWarning.ts`): with the whole team under a third of its HP, or nobody able to go, the cursor opens on NOT YET - and so it does on every card opened home from a raid until a step is taken (`BaseScene.homeOnTheRail`, `HubSceneData.justHome`), because SPACE mashed through the result screen opened the card and jumped; a press on NOT YET only closes it, so mashing opens and closes it for ever. The player is never stopped from jumping, keyboard included: Right from NOT YET is always JUMP IN, and an arrow that points at no other way in moves the cursor between the two buttons (`HubScene.handleKey`; only TAB, said nowhere, used to leave NOT YET - the second characters hunt, 11 Nov 2026). Which button the card opens on is `rimOpensOnNotYet()` in `hub/railWarning.ts`, and the floor's hint asks it too, so it says `[SPACE] LOOK DOWN` rather than promising a jump SPACE would not make. A pointer works the room as well: a click on a station or in the pit walks there and opens it (`BaseScene.pointAtHall`, `walkThen`). **A jump is a pure timeline** (`base/arenaDive.ts`, `world/raidArrival.ts`, each `*Pose(elapsedMs)`): the view back to the player, the held breath on the rail (`base/heldBreath.ts`: the fires drop, nothing is heard, what would be lost stamps across the screen under AT RISK - all of it for a team's first raids, `raidProgress.benchRaids`, then only a Pokémon at risk or a pack better than the Raid pack, and with neither no hold at all - and any key cuts it short, no third press), the hop with a horn and fire running round the hall (`base/ringOfFire.ts`, on the way out only), a flash, the house on its feet and the floor shaking, then the player and partner falling at twice their size (`fallerScale`) with wind and torn cloud, the raid's hunter coming over the rail after them and going on down past them, seen and not heard, because `hunterArrival` is the one word for a hunter entering the raid (`pursuerAt`, read off the plan's own rival), the hall rushing past with the floor going in far less than the rings (`ArenaView.diveFrame`), and the cloud (`cloudSheet`) shutting over it before `scene.start('world', { droppedIn: true })`, which opens under the same sheet and has them fall into a still picture with a thump and dust - free of the raid clock, as nothing runs but that (`WorldScene.advanceArrival`); nobody on the map reacts, because "!" means hunted; reduced motion is a plain fade. Whatever falls is on a camera of its own (`base/arenaDiveView.ts`), never zoomed: a magnified dither is a board of squares, and a Phaser camera added stays across scene restarts unless removed. **The rings and stands are drawn as only their inked squares** (`base/sparsePicture.ts`, a Blitter a picture): as whole images, eight mostly empty hall-sized pictures blended over every pixel of every frame, which with no graphics card halved the frame rate against the harbour's. A new big, mostly empty picture in the hall goes through `addSparsePicture`. **Nobody stands behind anything and one key does a side of the hall** (the captain, 1 Nov 2026: "I have to zigzag with the arrow keys the whole way"; `docs/screens/arena-flow/`). The pillars stand back against the wall with a gap at the door, what rises over the floor is solid under the part that would hide a figure (a pillar's shaft, `ArenaStation.rises`), and floor with one way in is taken away unless it fronts a station (`WALLED_OFF`). `base/arenaWalk.ts` is the walk: a key held against a stair of the oval goes on round it (`slideRound`, asked only in the hall by `BaseScene.roundTheStair`), never round anything pressed into on purpose, and a tap from standing only turns. The mat's left and right keys walk the runner's arms along the front of the stations; a torch at a station's end answers for it (`ArenaStation.past`, `RoomThing.alsoFaced`). `arenaWalk.test.ts` holds the routes and `tools/playtest/arenaFlow.mjs` counts them through the real scene: key changes, stops and tiles. The yard has one such stair of its own (`YARD_STAIR`), the foot of the Arena's east banner on the way every new player first walks. **All three tiles of the hall's mat lead out** (`ARENA_MAT_REACH`). **Round the hall stand the stands** (`base/arenaStands.ts`, painted at run time under the hall's layers, on negative coordinates, so the hall's grid and every tile fact are untouched): seats, a dim crowd of which a few shift by a pixel, an arcade and banners; nothing the camera can reach is left black but the way out: past the seats and the arcade `paintStands` lays the colosseum's roof to the picture's edge, which is the camera's bounds (`STANDS_WIDTH`/`STANDS_HEIGHT` follow the hall). **The pit is alive and keeps the player's clock**: a rare shadow and eyes in a gate (`base/pitLife.ts`, never explained anywhere in the game), and the floor and town lights by `windowLight` (`base/pitClock.ts`, lights only on walked ground). **Home is the same rim**: `homeAfterRaid` is `{ room: 'raid-arena', arrival: 'raid' }`, and the cloud over whatever the raid walked is laid back and thinned on a dither with the camera gone round to look when the raid gained at least `FOG_LIFT_LEAST_GAIN` of the map, and otherwise clears unwatched (`ArenaView.liftFogFromLastRaid`, compared with the picture kept at the jump, so a reload just shows the ground). The press that cuts a lift short also does what it was pressed for (`advanceFogLift` says which), so home, SPACE, ENTER costs no extra press. The Seagallop ties up and sounds her horn the first time the player is next out in the yard (`boatDueHome`), with what the raid brought out stood on the quay beside her (`base/haulOnTheQuay.ts`: a thing a kind, pictures only, for that one visit). The result screen stays, because it alone says what was secured, risked and spent, and says it of a lost raid. `tools/playtest/arenaHall.mjs` plays all of it; `walkIntoBase(page, 'raid-arena')` opens the bench, `openHallPanel` and `jumpIn` in `tools/playtest/deploy.mjs` are the rest.
- **THE RAID ARENA's crowd answers the player** (`base/arenaCrowd.ts`, its header the design; `docs/screens/arena-fun/`, and `docs/screens/arena-tidy/` for what the last sweep pared back). Its noises and the wind of a fall are the sound channel `hall`, so a partner's chirp or a world sound cannot cut them. How full the stands are is worked out from `raidRecord` (`crowdShare`; `crowdSeats` is every place, `seatedAt(share)` who is in, so a fuller house is the same people with more beside them), so `arenaStands.ts` paints the seats, the people (`paintCrowdLayer`, repainted when the share changes) and the banners (over anybody on their feet) as separate pictures. The result screen hands the raid's ending to the hall as `BaseSceneData.raidMood` (`moodOfRaid`): the view looks round at the seats for `CROWD_LOOK_MS` on a roar or a groan only (`crowdTakesTheView`; a step or any panel takes it back, and a pending fog lift waits behind it; neither adds a press to home-SPACE-ENTER) and the crowd roars with paper, cheers, claps or groans with some of them leaving and the fires low. The interact key at the outer wall (`isOuterWall`) waves and the nearest section waves back; going a quarter of the way round the outer floor runs a band of people beside the player (`LapWatch`), with the view left where it is, and on the rim nobody gets up, because that is choosing a way in; a whole lap brings the house up and the board says the time. `tools/playtest/arenaFun.mjs` plays it.
- **The hall keeps the run, and how close it was** (`base/runSlate.ts`, `runSlateView.ts`; `raidProgress.outRun`, optional, no version bump, kept by `recordRaidEnded` and broken by an abandoned raid too): a slate on the rail by the runner chalked `3 OUT` from the second raid out in a row, struck through in front of the groan when a raid is lost, then wiped. A raid out with the clock red or a hunter on its tail (`closeCallLine`, `world/raidMemory.ts`) gets the crowd to its feet whatever it hauled, says so on the result screen and the hall's board, and writes it as the flag's line in the pit. Both buy nothing.
- **A landing out of the pit can be stuck** (`world/raidArrival.ts`): a ring closes on the tile and the interact key as it shuts (`isCleanLanding`, one press a landing) gets a sharper thump, wider dust, no crouch, a bigger bounce from the partner and a board over the player's head. It buys nothing, moves no time, and a miss is the landing as it always was. `raidProgress.landings` (`SaveManager.recordLanding`, optional, no version bump) is only for pride: the board and the marshal. A stepped driver presses a frame early (`arenaFun.mjs`): a 100ms frame can step over the window. The arrow keys drift whoever is falling (`steered`), for the feel of it.
- **The pit remembers the raids that went down into it** (`world/raidMemory.ts`; `raidProgress.raidMemory`, optional, no version bump): a cross or a flag where each of the last dozen ended, faded by age, and the last raid's walk as a dotted line, drawn on the floor's own layer by `ArenaView.drawMemory`. The walk rides on `ActiveRunSession.walk`, outdoors only, and goes home with `recordRaidEnded` from each ending; a mark's line is written then and never worked out again, because the Pokemon it names may be long gone. At the rail the rim names the mark in view nearest the player (`nameTheMark`). A map is smaller than the hole, so from one rail only the near half of it is on the screen: a rule about what is looked at asks what is in view, not `pitLookPoint`.
- **A coin can go over the rail, and nothing explains it** (`base/pitCoins.ts`, key C at the rim, named only until first used): ₽10 out of the vault, a long wait, a plink; eyes in a gate look at whoever threw it. After more and more coins (`coinsForGift`) something comes up out of the pit and lies by the runner until walked on to (`raidProgress.pit`: coins and how many picked up, optional, no version bump, so what is due is derived). `pitCoins.test.ts` holds every gift to cosmetic and humble - common furniture or an everyday hat, never a prize - and dearer in coins than in the shop.
- **Bouts for show: the next hunter stands on the hall's far floor and will fight for nothing** (`base/arenaBouts.ts`, `base/boutLadder.ts`, each rival's `hall` lines in `world/hunters.ts`). **A bout can never write to the save, by construction**: the real `BattleScene` with `exhibition` set and no run session (`create` refuses both), on deep copies of the bench through the save's own serialiser (`boutParty`), a copy of the rack and no experience, and the only code in that scene that touches a save is `resolveRunWipe`, behind a run session - `scenes/exhibitionBout.test.ts` fails a second place that names one, and `tools/playtest/arenaFun.mjs --only=bout` compares the save byte for byte. A new thing a fight writes must stay behind `runSession`. **Everybody on the far floor has one tile, every day**: a gym leader the player has met stands on their own (`LEADER_TILES`, by gym order) and offers the bout from there on the day they are the rival too (`rivalTile`), and only a rival with no gym met takes `RIVAL_TILE` - when a met leader stepped onto the rival's tile for their day, a bout that moved the ladder on walked her back to her own and put the next rival where she had stood (the owner, 12 Nov 2026: "Misty and the green NPC had swapped places"). Ask `rivalTile`, never `RIVAL_TILE`, for where the rival is. All that goes home is which way it went (`BaseSceneData.bout`), and the hall writes the one field, `raidProgress.bouts` (optional, no version bump): who has been beaten in a row, and how often all five have been, which hangs the player's banner. Walking up turns the view to the wall and the crowd (`nearTheRival`); YIELD is a loss, so a bout with something to lose (the ladder here; a run of wins, a cup or rental run, or the day's paying rental run in THE BATTLE ARENA) asks "Yield the bout?" on NO first, with the room's words for the stake on `HallBout`/`DojoBout.stake` (`arena/yieldStake.ts`): it sits one press right of FIGHT. A question in the box must fit one line or it lies over its answers, so a rival's offer is said first and `BOUT_QUESTION` asked after.
- **THE RAID ARENA's door plays a trailer before its hall, once, the first time through it after a first raid** (the captain, 2026-10-29: "when I walk into the gym for the first time ... an epic cutscene of me in a raid"; held back past the first raid on 1 Nov 2026, when he asked for fewer barriers to one): `cutscene/arenaIntro.ts` is the script and its rules (cast, every word, beat times), `cutscene/arenaIntroForest.ts` the wood it is set in, `scenes/ArenaIntroScene.ts` plays it with the game's own art - the player in their own look (`lookTexture`, as the harbour draws them) and their name, their partner in the fight, their next raid's hunter (`rivalForRaid`) and that hunter's team, a comic panel for Charizard. Unasked that once (`raidProgress.arenaIntroSeen`, written as it starts by `SaveManager.watchArenaIntro`, optional, no version bump); after that the door is a door like any other, and the Gym Guide beside it asks WATCH THE REEL AGAIN? once he has said the day's work (`BaseScene.offerTheReel`), which plays it with a SKIP chip. Any key or click past `ARENA_INTRO_ARMING_MS` ends it in the great hall, where the door goes. Who says the closing line is `ARENA_INTRO_HOST` (the Gym Guide until the captain says otherwise). **Its motion runs on the scene clock (`move`), never `this.tweens`**: Phaser's tween manager keeps the wall clock, so under a stepped loop or a slow frame every tween drifts off the `this.time` cues it was choreographed against. Big words are `ui/stampedLettering.ts`, the title logo's own stamping. `walkIntoBase` presses through it; `tools/playtest/arenaIntro.mjs` films every beat and checks once, skip and the loadout.
